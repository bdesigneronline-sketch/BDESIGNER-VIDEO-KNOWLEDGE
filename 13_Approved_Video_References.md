# Reference direction and asset inventory

## Approved editorial preferences
- User prefers **After Effects-style professional motion graphics**, not static slides.
- Storyboards are **9:16** by default.
- Inter typographic system.
- Small top-left category label and top-right scene counter.
- Slightly smaller headlines than early drafts; fewer words per sentence.
- Lower compact subtitles, consistent across every screen.
- Small logo (never oversized).
- Light and dark coordinated screens; opening can be light then dark.
- Construction model hero visuals; polished transitions, music, SFX, professional voiceover.
- Generate **one screen at a time** for visual approval, then produce separate scene animation prompts.
- External creative references and user-uploaded teaser frames exist in prior conversations but **are not bundled here**. Do not claim to have viewed an asset unless attached.

## Assets needed before final production
- Official BDESIGNER logo in approved size/variant.
- Latest verified plugin screenshots and short viewport screen recordings.
- Product UI captures (stage selectors, preview, BOQ, export).
- Approved light/dark style frames.
- Approved motion reference clips.
- Verified public pricing/CTA wording, if sale is mentioned.
- Final release status for depicted features.

## Asset-safe procedure
References can guide composition and pacing, but do not clone third-party copyrighted music, voice, imagery, logo or transitions exactly. Create original execution in BDESIGNER branding.

## Asset manifest template
For each clip: ID | feature/stage | real footage vs concept | build/version | verified date | approved for publication? | destination scene | notes.
