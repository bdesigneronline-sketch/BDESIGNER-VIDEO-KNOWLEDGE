# Voiceover, subtitles and sound design

## Voice direction
- Clear, warm, professional and confident; accessible to Indian architects, civil engineers and contractors.
- Natural English by default unless requested otherwise.
- Short phrases with breathing room, conversational rather than announcer-heavy.
- No inflated or unverifiable claims.
- Narration complements visuals; avoid repeating every visible label word-for-word.

## Subtitle placement and style
- Bottom caption lane, positioned above platform interface safe area.
- Inter regular/medium; compact and legible; one short idea at a time.
- High contrast in light/dark scenes and consistent alignment.
- Use exact approved terms: "BOQ", "SketchUp", "MEP — Beta", "Finishing — Beta".
- Avoid large rectangular blocks that compete with model visuals.

## Sound design
- Subtle low-impact thumps for structural elements appearing.
- Soft UI clicks for toggles or step changes.
- Clean whooshes with model/camera moves.
- Tiny data ticks when quantities connect to BOQ.
- Satisfying paper-like reveal for PDF export.
- Music: premium modern tech bed, restrained; never drown speech.
- Pace and SFX synchronized to animation, not sprinkled randomly.

## Audio deliverable from Claude
Supply: full read-aloud script, sentence timing estimates, emotion/tone cues, pronunciation notes, music mood, SFX cue list and final mix considerations.

## Voiceover example
"Design is the start. Construction needs more. Model each stage. Review quantities. Build your BOQ. BDESIGNER — build construction, not just geometry."
