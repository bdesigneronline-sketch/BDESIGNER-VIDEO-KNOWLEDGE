## Release-claim states
- **Approved scope:** intended product feature, not a shipping claim.
- **Verified in demo:** recorded working in a particular build, not necessarily publicly available.
- **Available:** owner confirms deployed product release.
- **Beta:** public beta clearly labelled, if released.
- **In development:** being built, not yet advertised as usable.
- **Planned:** idea or roadmap, not promised date.
