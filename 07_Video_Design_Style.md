# Video design style — approved creative direction

## Core format
- Default **9:16 vertical** for social teasers, shorts and reels; use 16:9 for full tutorials when requested.
- Premium, clean technology film / After Effects-style motion graphics.
- Editorial restraint: strong one-line statement, excellent typography, model/product as hero.
- Do **not** make a slideshow of static AI images.

## Repeatable screen anatomy
- Top left: very small category/section label.
- Top right: subtle scene counter or progress marker.
- Center: one dominant visual; a construction stage model, UI crop or abstract form.
- Main headline: short, usually 2–6 words; small enough to avoid overwhelming the hero visual.
- Bottom: compact subtitle/voice-caption zone with clear safe margins.
- Small official logo only when appropriate (often end slate), never large.
- Keep positioning consistent across sequential frames.

## Light/dark sequence
- Light screen: warm off-white/soft white base, crisp charcoal text, precise maroon accents.
- Dark screen: near-black/charcoal background, off-white text, restrained maroon and gold accents.
- Screen 1 can be light and screen 2 dark for the opening contrast, with intentional alternation later.

## Typography
- Inter, regular/medium/semibold/bold weights.
- Strong hierarchy, but reduce overall caption and header size compared with crowded reference treatments.
- On-screen headline: ideally one short phrase; no multiline paragraph.
- Lower subtitles: one compact thought at a time; short, readable, synchronized.

## Example shot layout
Label: "BDESIGNER / STAGES" | Counter: "03 / 08"
Hero: authentic SketchUp structure model with dynamic camera move
Headline: "From Model to BOQ"
Subtitle: "Quantities, connected to your model."

## Don't
Oversized logos; verbose headlines; watermarks; needless UI overlays; confusing labels; fake feature screenshots; glitchy wipes; bright borders or inaccessible dark-mode text; generic footage that cannot be distinguished from the actual product.
