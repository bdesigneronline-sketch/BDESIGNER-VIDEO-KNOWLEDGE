## Tutorial production format
Learning goal -> prerequisites -> on-screen navigation -> one practical example -> expected result -> common mistake -> closing recap. Use zoom/pointer effects sparingly and keep inputs readable.

## Safety/technical accuracy
No statement that a tutorial replaces engineering design, site verification, applicable standards or professional supervision. Stage sizes, rates and counts must be clearly illustrative unless project-grounded.
