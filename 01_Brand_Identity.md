# Brand identity — Bdesigner Tool

## Core names
- Product: **Bdesigner Tool** or **BDESIGNER Tool** in headline designs.
- Category: construction-focused **SketchUp plugin**.
- Website: **Bdesignertool.com**.
- Positioning line: **Build Construction, Not Just Geometry.**
- Related but separate services company: BDesigner.online.

## Visual foundation
- Clean, premium architecture/software aesthetic, confident and practical; avoid noisy 3D gaming motifs.
- Typography: **Inter**; small, sharp, confident labels with clear hierarchy.
- Primary maroon/red from established brand references: **#701B19**.
- Restrained highlight gold from established references: **#C9A24A**.
- Pair with charcoal/near-black and warm neutral white. Use accessible contrast.
- Both light and dark screen treatments are approved design directions; a typical sequence can open with light then alternate to dark for emphasis.
- Logo: small, intentional placement. **Never oversized**. Source official master logo when supplied; do not invent/redraw it.
- Consistent left alignment and generous negative space.

## Copy identity
- Technical enough for architects, civil engineers and contractors, but understandable in seconds.
- Use real benefits: stage-based construction elements, model-linked quantities, required works, rates, BOQ and PDF export.
- Short headline examples: "Model the Work.", "Quantities, Connected.", "Build Beyond Geometry.", "From Model to BOQ."
- Avoid: exaggerated AI claims, guaranteed cost accuracy, invented speed claims, vague "one-click complete construction" language.

## Brand separation
Architectural/construction services at **BDesigner.online** must not be presented as a capability inside the plugin.
