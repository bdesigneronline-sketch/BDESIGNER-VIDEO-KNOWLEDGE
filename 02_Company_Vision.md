# Product vision for the story

## Why Bdesigner Tool exists
A SketchUp model can show geometry beautifully, yet practical construction workflows also need staged elements, quantities, applicable work items, rates, BOQ and useful outputs. Bdesigner Tool aims to connect modelling and quantity-based construction planning inside a familiar SketchUp environment.

## Story to tell on screen
1. Standard problem: 3D geometry and construction quantities are often disconnected.
2. Product idea: build recognized construction elements by stage, not just generic shapes.
3. Connected workflow: model -> required works -> pricing -> quantities -> BOQ -> PDF.
4. Benefit: clearer, more practical workflow for AEC professionals and their estimates.
5. Close: credible product footage and a restrained call to explore the tool.

## Editorial guardrail
This is a **product intent and approved marketing story**, not a certification of structural design, field execution, regulatory compliance or independently measured outcomes. Avoid "eliminates all errors", "perfect estimate" or "fully automatic engineering" claims.

## Audience/market perspective
India-first construction workflow examples: foundation, structural, masonry, water tanks, stairs, rates, measurement formats. Generic/global terms remain appropriate when they apply. Never assume particular local rates without source and date.
