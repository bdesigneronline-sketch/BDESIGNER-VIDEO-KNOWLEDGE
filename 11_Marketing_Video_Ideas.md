## Creative campaign rule
Deliver individual screen 1–8 + end-frame prompts when producing a multi-scene cinematic teaser. For the opening, a light first screen and dark second screen are welcome, but keep all screens part of one consistent design family.

## Content sequences
- Awareness: brand positioning + clear vision.
- Proof: current product in SketchUp doing a real workflow.
- Education: short per-stage demos.
- Conversion: pricing only with current offer confirmation.
- Retention: feature updates and practical workflows.

## Before release
Mark any planned/beta feature appropriately, especially slab refinements, MEP — Beta and Finishing — Beta; check current tested build.
